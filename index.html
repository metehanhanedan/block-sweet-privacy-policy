Block Sweet – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24.11.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Sweet ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

a) Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address
  • Gameplay data and interactions
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

b) Advertising Data (Unity Ads)

Our game uses Unity Ads to show optional rewarded video ads. Unity Ads may collect Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, and ad interactions.

c) Leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services)

The Game signs you in anonymously with Unity Authentication, which creates a random player ID; we do not ask for your email or a password. If you take part in the leaderboards, the player name you choose and your scores are sent to Unity Leaderboards and are shown publicly to other players. Do not use your real name or personal information as your player name.

d) In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (star packs and "No Ads"). Payments are processed by Google Play; we never receive or store your card or payment details. The Game receives the purchased item and an order/transaction ID from Google Play, which are used only to deliver the item and to restore "No Ads" on a new installation. Purchases are stored on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • Improve game performance
  • Fix bugs
  • Analyze usage
  • Show optional rewarded ads
  • Show leaderboards
  • Deliver and restore in-app purchases

3. Third-Party Services

We use Unity Ads, Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Leaderboards), Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play Services (including Google Play Billing). Each service processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Data is shared only with the services listed above, as needed to provide the Game.

5. Children's Privacy

Not intended for children under 13.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data. Data sent to the services above is encrypted in transit.

7. User Rights

Users can request data deletion, including their leaderboard name and scores, by emailing us at the address below. Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your device; purchases remain in your Google Play account.
